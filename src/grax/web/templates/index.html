About GraxPy

Developed at Helmholtz-Zentrum Berlin (HZB), GraxPy is a tool for calculating grating diffraction efficiencies and is open to collaboration. It offers two solvers: modal RCWA and the Nevière differential method.

Simulate laminar, blazed, and sinusoidal gratings, or upload and process an AFM-measured profile. Surface roughness can be represented either with a Debye–Waller efficiency correction or with explicitly simulated random interfaces.
