A coated grating is used at grazing incidence (total external reflection or the multilayer Bragg peak) and is solved with RCWA or the Nevière method; the bare crystal is solved with Takagi-Taupin in the crystal Bragg reflection. The solver is chosen when you scan. Each layer follows the profile below it; a planarization length ν above 0 smooths it while the layer grows (a Fourier component q is damped by exp(−ν q² t) across thickness t), which rounds the finger steps but needs many more solver slices.

Deformation depth

The penetration depth is the depth over which the deformation of the fundamental period decays; finer harmonics decay faster. The preview amplitude only sets the deformation drawn above (exaggerated in the side view); the amplitudes of a scan are chosen on the grating's page.
